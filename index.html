How ExportIQ works

1. Create a shipment
Enter exporter, product and shipping information.
2. Generate draft documents
Choose a document type and create a draft from the shipment profile.
3. Verify consistency
Compare important values across documents and resolve mismatches.
4. Review compliance
Use the preliminary checklist as a starting point, then verify official requirements.
This is a front-end prototype with local demo interactions. It does not store data on a server, submit customs filings, or provide certified legal advice.
